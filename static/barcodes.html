Workbench Display — QR Samples

Each QR encodes a full ISO-15434 label payload, exactly as the Zebra DS3678 emits it (82301 is the client's real string). The server reads the 5-digit group after the P data identifier to choose the spec image shown here. On the live line the DS3678 feeds the display over the serial splitter; to test here, scan a code with a phone barcode-scanner-keyboard app into the /station/WB-01 window, or print this sheet.
